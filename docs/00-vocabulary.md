# 0. Vocabulary you must force the team to learn

**Intent**
The real purpose behind the work. Not "what the ticket says," but "what problem are we trying to solve?"

**Acceptance criteria**
The concrete conditions that prove the work is complete.

**Source of truth**
The original reliable reference: customer request, product decision, incident, ADR, design, regulation, metric, or stakeholder confirmation.

**Assumption**
Something we believe but have not proven.

**Constraint**
A limitation we must respect: deadline, architecture, security, compliance, performance, compatibility.

**Verification evidence**
Proof that the work was checked: tests, screenshots, logs, manual steps, links, review notes.

**Definition of Ready**
The minimum quality a ticket must have before developers start work.

**Definition of Done**
The minimum quality work must reach before it is accepted as finished.

**Accountability**
The human owner remains responsible, even if AI helped write the ticket, code, review, or documentation.
